# Citas — Tantra y Práctica Avanzada

> «The crux of tantric, or vajrayana, mahamudra is the activation of one's subtlest level of mind and subsequent contemplation by this consciousness of the ultimate nature of that very mind itself.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Clear Light of Bliss* [[clear-light-of-bliss-mahamudra-in-vajrayana-buddhism-a-kelsang-gyatso-]], Back cover

> «In tantra we are dealing with pleasure, not with pain. The person who is qualified to practice tantra is able to cope with pleasure, to experience pleasure without losing control, to utilize it. This is the essential characteristic of the tantric personality.»
> — **Lama Thubten Yeshe**, *The Bliss of Inner Fire* [[the-bliss-of-inner-fire-heart-practice-of-the-six-yogas-of-thubten-yes]], Cap. 1

> «La esencia de la práctica del Highest Yoga Tantra es generar una mente de gran dicha espontánea y usar esa mente dichosa para meditar en la vacuidad.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *The New Guide to Dakini Land* [[the-new-guide-to-dakini-land-the-highest-yoga-tantra-kelsang-gyatso-19]]

## Por autor

### Lama Thubten Yeshe (Fuego Interior)

> «Inner fire is the real chocolate! Whereas you may find it difficult to get results with other meditations, inner fire is a sensitive, quick way to convince yourself that you are progressing. It will surprise you.»
> — **Lama Thubten Yeshe**, *The Bliss of Inner Fire* [[the-bliss-of-inner-fire-heart-practice-of-the-six-yogas-of-thubten-yes]], Cap. 1

> «Even if you could stay in samadhi meditation twenty-four hours a day for twenty days, Milarepa would say to you, "That means nothing! It does not compare to my inner fire meditation." [...] Inner fire is incomparable.»
> — **Lama Thubten Yeshe**, *The Bliss of Inner Fire* [[the-bliss-of-inner-fire-heart-practice-of-the-six-yogas-of-thubten-yes]], Cap. 1

> «Look at Milarepa. I doubt that you have created more negative karma than he did; he killed many people when he was young. But because of his inner strength, he was also able to develop perfect renunciation, perfect bodhicitta, perfect right view, perfect Six Yogas of Naropa. He said goodbye to samsara.»
> — **Lama Thubten Yeshe**, *The Bliss of Inner Fire* [[the-bliss-of-inner-fire-heart-practice-of-the-six-yogas-of-thubten-yes]], Cap. 1

> «You cannot get olive oil by squeezing sand. My inner fire meditation is incomparable.»
> — **Milarepa** a Gampopa, citado en *The Bliss of Inner Fire* [[the-bliss-of-inner-fire-heart-practice-of-the-six-yogas-of-thubten-yes]], Cap. 17

> «The human body is the gold mine of tantra; it is our most precious possession.»
> — **Lama Thubten Yeshe**, *The Bliss of Inner Fire* [[the-bliss-of-inner-fire-heart-practice-of-the-six-yogas-of-thubten-yes]], Cap. 1

> «Put Mickey Mouse on your altar if you find it helpful. Use whatever communicates with you and helps you to grow.»
> — **Lama Thubten Yeshe**, *The Bliss of Inner Fire* [[the-bliss-of-inner-fire-heart-practice-of-the-six-yogas-of-thubten-yes]], Cap. 21

> «When you touch water, feel it is blissful; when you see a fire, imagine that it is the inner fire; when you see light, imagine that it is kundalini energy.»
> — **Lama Thubten Yeshe**, *The Bliss of Inner Fire* [[the-bliss-of-inner-fire-heart-practice-of-the-six-yogas-of-thubten-yes]], Cap. 22

### Geshe Kelsang Gyatso (Vajrayogini / Dakini Land)

> «Las instrucciones sobre la práctica de Vajrayogini son una síntesis de todas las instrucciones esenciales contenidas en los Tantras de Heruka, Yamantaka y Guhyasamaja. Todos los puntos esenciales de las etapas del Mantra Secreto están incluidos dentro de la práctica de Vajrayogini.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *The New Guide to Dakini Land* [[the-new-guide-to-dakini-land-the-highest-yoga-tantra-kelsang-gyatso-19]]

> «Así como el fuego que es producido por la madera termina consumiendo la madera que lo produjo, así también la dicha tántrica, que se desarrolla a partir del apego deseoso, termina consumiendo el apego deseoso que la originó. Este método hábil de transformar el apego en el camino espiritual fue adoptado por Maestros como Ghantapa y Tilopa.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *The New Guide to Dakini Land* [[the-new-guide-to-dakini-land-the-highest-yoga-tantra-kelsang-gyatso-19]]

> «Heruka es la manifestación de la mente dichosa de todos los Buddhas, y Vajrayogini es la manifestación de la mente de sabiduría de todos los Buddhas. Dado que estas dos mentes son una sola entidad, Heruka y Vajrayogini son una sola persona que muestra aspectos diferentes; no son como una pareja ordinaria.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *The New Guide to Dakini Land* [[the-new-guide-to-dakini-land-the-highest-yoga-tantra-kelsang-gyatso-19]]

> «La fase de generación es una realización de un yoga creativo que se alcanza como resultado de una concentración pura en traer los tres cuerpos al camino, en la que nos generamos mentalmente como una deidad tántrica y nuestro entorno como el mandala de la deidad.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *The New Guide to Dakini Land* [[the-new-guide-to-dakini-land-the-highest-yoga-tantra-kelsang-gyatso-19]]

> «Si revelamos abiertamente nuestra práctica a otros, las bendiciones que recibimos durante las empoderamientos se disiparán. Hablar abiertamente sobre nuestras experiencias de meditación es una falta.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *The New Guide to Dakini Land* [[the-new-guide-to-dakini-land-the-highest-yoga-tantra-kelsang-gyatso-19]]

### Geshe Kelsang Gyatso (Clear Light of Bliss / Mahamudra)

> «Using such simultaneous great bliss to realize emptiness was the essential heart practice of the great secret mantra masters of ancient India such as Saraha, Nagarjuna, Tilopa, Naropa, Maitripa and so forth. It was also the heart meditation of such great Tibetan masters as Marpa, Milarepa, Gampopa, Je Tsong-khapa and so forth.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Clear Light of Bliss* [[clear-light-of-bliss-mahamudra-in-vajrayana-buddhism-a-kelsang-gyatso-]], Introduction

> «The yoga of inner fire pervades all completion stage practices. It is the trunk from which all such practices stem and is therefore referred to as the foundation of the completion stage.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Clear Light of Bliss* [[clear-light-of-bliss-mahamudra-in-vajrayana-buddhism-a-kelsang-gyatso-]], Inner Fire

> «To compare other methods to inner fire is like comparing a donkey to a fine horse.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Clear Light of Bliss* [[clear-light-of-bliss-mahamudra-in-vajrayana-buddhism-a-kelsang-gyatso-]], Inner Fire

> «Nagarjuna stated that if one does not realize the illusory body then there is no purpose in studying or practising either sutra or secret mantra.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Clear Light of Bliss* [[clear-light-of-bliss-mahamudra-in-vajrayana-buddhism-a-kelsang-gyatso-]], The Illusory Body

### Panchen Lama I y Ghantapa (textos raíz)

> «Si no negamos todas las cosas que normalmente vemos, / nuestra visión de la vacuidad es pura invención nuestra; / no tiene función para resolver nuestro problema de las aflicciones.»
> — **Norsang Gyatso** (citado por el Panchen Lama), en *The Oral Instructions of Mahamudra* [[the-oral-instructions-of-mahamudra-the-very-essence-of-geshe-kelsang-g]]

> «Deberíamos meditar unipuntualmente / en la gota indestructible que siempre mora en nuestro corazón. / Quienes se familiaricen con esta meditación / desarrollarán definitivamente la sabiduría exaltada.»
> — **Ghantapa**, citado en *The Oral Instructions of Mahamudra* [[the-oral-instructions-of-mahamudra-the-very-essence-of-geshe-kelsang-g]]

> «La vacuidad que se explica en las enseñanzas del Sutra de Buda / y la gran beatitud que se explica en las enseñanzas del Tantra de Buda — / la unión de estos dos es la esencia misma de las 84.000 enseñanzas de Buda. / ¡Que florezca para siempre la doctrina del Conquistador Losang Dragpa!»
> — **Gungtang**, en *The Oral Instructions of Mahamudra* [[the-oral-instructions-of-mahamudra-the-very-essence-of-geshe-kelsang-g]]

### Pabongkha Rinpoche (Vajrayogini)

> «As the times become more degenerate, the blessings of other deities slow down. As for Heruka Father and Mother, in degenerate times, their blessings become greater and swifter.»
> — **Pabongkha Rinpoche**, *The Extremely Secret Dakini of Naropa* [[the-extremely-secret-dakini-of-naropa-vajrayogini-practice-pabongkha-d]]

> «Furthermore, in modern times such as these, even if we don't know anything else, attainments will be accomplished through mere recitation with faith. Nowhere else is it said that you can accomplish attainments through merely reading.»
> — **Pabongkha Rinpoche**, *The Extremely Secret Dakini of Naropa* [[the-extremely-secret-dakini-of-naropa-vajrayogini-practice-pabongkha-d]]

> «Concerning emptiness and bodhichitta, they are like the two wings of a bird by which it flies through the sky; therefore the two accumulations follow after these two and result in the attainment of the two buddha-bodies.»
> — **Pabongkha Rinpoche**, *The Extremely Secret Dakini of Naropa* [[the-extremely-secret-dakini-of-naropa-vajrayogini-practice-pabongkha-d]], Yoga of the Immeasurables

> «Don't strive for mere heat and bliss; you should primarily collect the winds within the central channel for the sake of generating the realization of bliss and emptiness.»
> — **Pabongkha Rinpoche**, *The Extremely Secret Dakini of Naropa* [[the-extremely-secret-dakini-of-naropa-vajrayogini-practice-pabongkha-d]], The Stages of Progressing on the Path

> «The perception on the side of appearance in Mantra and the view on the side of emptiness must be unified. [...] Without the view and so forth, meditation on a deity is no more special than what the non-Buddhists and the Bon practice.»
> — **Pabongkha Rinpoche**, *The Extremely Secret Dakini of Naropa* [[the-extremely-secret-dakini-of-naropa-vajrayogini-practice-pabongkha-d]]

### Geshe Kelsang Gyatso (Tantric Grounds and Paths)

> «Para que los seres vivientes alcancen la gran liberación, o la iluminación completa, Buda reveló dos caminos: el camino común y el camino incomún.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Tantric Grounds and Paths* [[tantric-grounds-and-paths-how-to-enter-progress-on-and-geshe-kelsang-g]]

> «El Vajrayana [...] es llamado a veces el "Vehículo del Apego" porque, en vez de intentar abandonar el apego de inmediato, los practicantes de este vehículo usan el apego como ayuda para generar gran dicha espontánea, con la que luego meditan en la vacuidad.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Tantric Grounds and Paths* [[tantric-grounds-and-paths-how-to-enter-progress-on-and-geshe-kelsang-g]]

> «Cuando el Dharma de Buda Shakyamuni esté a punto de terminar, la práctica del Mantra Secreto florecerá brevemente de forma muy amplia en este mundo, como la llama de una vela brilla intensamente justo antes de apagarse por completo.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Tantric Grounds and Paths* [[tantric-grounds-and-paths-how-to-enter-progress-on-and-geshe-kelsang-g]]

### Jetsun Drakpa Gyeltsen (Once Yogas)

> «Sleeping, Waking, and Tasting Nectar, / Yoga of Immeasurables, Guru Yoga, and / Generating Oneself as the Deity, / The Yoga of Purifying Beings, / Receiving Blessings from the Heroes and Heroines, / Verbal and Mental Recitation, / The Inconceivable Yoga, and / The Yoga of Regular Activities; / These are the Eleven Yogas.»
> — **Jetsun Drakpa Gyeltsen** (patriarca Sakya), citado en *Sublime Path to Kechara Paradise* [[sublime-path-to-kechara-paradise-vajrayogini-s-eleven-yogas-commentary]]

> «Just closing your eyes and reciting these words is not taking refuge. The right way to take refuge is to think about your own and all other sentient beings' problems, to recognize that you need a protector, and to realize that the only infallible protector is the Three Jewels.»
> — *Sublime Path to Kechara Paradise* [[sublime-path-to-kechara-paradise-vajrayogini-s-eleven-yogas-commentary]], Yoga of the Immeasurables

> «Tibetans practice a hundred deities, but do not see a single one.»
> — *Expresión tibetana*, citada en *Sublime Path to Kechara Paradise* [[sublime-path-to-kechara-paradise-vajrayogini-s-eleven-yogas-commentary]]

### Milarepa y maestros tántricos

> «Debes saber que la naturaleza de todas las apariencias es mente y que la naturaleza de la mente es vacuidad.»
> — **Milarepa**, citado en *Budismo Moderno (Tantra)* [[budismo-moderno-el-camino-de-la-compasion-y-la-sabiduria-tantra]], Cap. 1

> «Primero, debido al miedo a la muerte, corrí hacia el Dharma. Luego, me adiestré en el estado de la inmortalidad. Finalmente, comprendí que la muerte no existe y me relajé.»
> — **Yogui anónimo**, citado en *Budismo Moderno (Tantra)* [[budismo-moderno-el-camino-de-la-compasion-y-la-sabiduria-tantra]], Cap. 3

> «El secreto supremo del gran gozo surge al derretirse las gotas dentro del canal central; por lo tanto, resulta difícil encontrar en el mundo una persona que experimente este gozo.»
> — **Buda**, *Tantra raíz conciso de Heruka*, en *Budismo Moderno (Tantra)* [[budismo-moderno-el-camino-de-la-compasion-y-la-sabiduria-tantra]], Cap. 6

> «La unión de la vacuidad que se expone en las enseñanzas del sutra de Buda y el gran gozo que se expone en sus enseñanzas tántricas es la esencia de las ochenta y cuatro mil enseñanzas de Buda.»
> — **Erudito Gungtang**, en *Budismo Moderno (Tantra)* [[budismo-moderno-el-camino-de-la-compasion-y-la-sabiduria-tantra]], Cap. 4

### Budismo Moderno (Volumen 3)

> «Tara es un Buda femenino, una manifestación de la sabiduría última de todos los Budas. […] Tara significa "Liberadora", y recibe este nombre porque nos libera de los ocho temores externos… y de los ocho internos.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Budismo Moderno, Volumen 3* [[budismo-moderno-volumen-3]]

> «Por muchas razones la práctica de Vajrayoguini es idónea para los tiempos presentes. Si confiamos en ella con sinceridad, un buen corazón y fe, es definitivo que podamos alcanzar la iluminación total.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Budismo Moderno, Volumen 3* [[budismo-moderno-volumen-3]]

> «Avalokiteshvara, o Chenrezig en tibetano, es un ser iluminado y la manifestación de la compasión de todos los Budas, y se le conoce como el Buda de la Compasión.»
> — **Geshe Kelsang Gyatso**, *Budismo Moderno, Volumen 3* [[budismo-moderno-volumen-3]]

### Tsarchen Losel Gyatso

> «Of all the worldly and transcendent forms of illusion / In which the pageant of limitless Conquerors' blissful wisdom can appear, / Now it has arisen here as the sublime Queen of Kechara. / I keep you in my heart; please hold me in your blissful embrace.»
> — **Tsarchen Losel Gyatso**, «Prayer to See the Glorious Face of the Queen of Kechara», en *Sublime Path to Kechara Paradise* [[sublime-path-to-kechara-paradise-vajrayogini-s-eleven-yogas-commentary]]
